# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Students and individuals needing synchronized focus, accountability, and gamified study rooms.

## Product Purpose
To provide a premium real-time Study OS that transforms solo studying into an immersive, multiplayer, and gamified experience.

## Brand Personality
Adaptable across themes (currently focused on exploring):
1. Minimal, professional, and focused (Monochrome white)
2. Dark, cinematic, and immersive
3. Vibrant, energetic, and gamified

## Anti-references
- SaaS clichés (e.g., hero-metric templates).
- Over-used generic glassmorphism as a default decoration.
- Excessive or "insanely rounded" corners (e.g., 32px+ radii on standard cards).

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence**: The UI should feel like a serious, production-grade tool for people who take their craft seriously.
- **Show, Don't Tell**: Let the live data (active timers, global chat) speak for the energy of the room rather than relying on decorative UI scaffolding.
- **Intentional Interaction**: Every animation and micro-interaction must serve the workflow, not distract from it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Standard web accessibility (WCAG AA compliance).
- High contrast text (especially on dark or tinted backgrounds, strictly adhering to ≥4.5:1 ratios).
- Reduced motion support (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`) for all animations.
